# Afriment Market Entry Strategy & B2B Pipeline Analysis

**Role:** Assistant Team Lead | Pod C (Team BrainBox)
**Timeline:** 4-Week Corporate Simulation

## Executive Summary
This repository serves as a consulting case study detailing a 4-week intensive data analytics sprint for Afriment. Operating without pre-cleaned datasets, my team engineered raw market intelligence into a comprehensive market entry blueprint. The final deliverables include a robust B2B funding pipeline, a skills demand-vs-supply gap analysis, and competitive UX platform teardowns.

---

## The Business Challenge
Afriment required a data-backed market entry strategy, curriculum architecture, and partnership outreach matrix. The objective was to identify unmet market demand, secure high-yield B2B funding leads, and optimize Afriment's social authority to drive cohort acquisition.

---

## Technical Methodology & Execution
* **Data Engineering (Power Query):** Executed rigorous ETL pipelines to normalize unstructured 12-month social media metrics, trim hidden string anomalies, and standardize categorical data across 117 competitor course offerings.
* **Data Modeling (DAX):** Built relational data models and custom DAX measures to calculate per-follower engagement rates, strategic fit scores for foundation partners, and synchronized slicers across multiple reporting views.
* **Visual Strategy:** Designed executive-facing reports using a custom dark slate navy, emerald green, and metallic gold aesthetic to focus stakeholder attention on critical KPIs and actionable insights.

## Key Strategic Deliverables

### 1. The Market Gap: Skills Demand vs. Supply
*Identified the most lucrative curriculum expansion opportunities by plotting employer job demand against existing market course supply.*

![Demand vs Supply Scatter Plot](assets/slides/Slide_Demand_Supply.png)

**Strategic Outcome:** Business Analysis and Project Management were identified as immediate "Hot Cakes," sitting far above the 50% employer demand line. No-code and Automation presented a massive greenfield opportunity with zero verified market supply in the scrape.

### 2. Platform UX & Competitor Teardown
*Evaluated 50 ed-tech competitors across African markets to isolate Afriment's unique value proposition.*

![Competitor Teardown Matrix](assets/slides/Slide_Competitor_Teardown.png)

**Strategic Outcome:** Discovered that while competitors lean heavily on public employer-facing portals, Afriment's ultimate competitive moat is its managed, real-company pod execution. Recommended pivoting marketing collateral to emphasize this risk-free talent discovery pipeline.

### 3. B2B Pipeline & Foundation Funding
*Mapped 60 global foundations and 38 career communities to build an immediate partnership outreach matrix.*

![Funding and Community Pipeline](assets/slides/Slide_Funding_Pipeline.png)

**Strategic Outcome:** Prioritized 24 Tier-1 community partners holding 95% of the reachable target audience (28,000+ learners) for immediate co-hosted portfolio teardowns. Isolated the Prince Casper and Whitefield Foundations for immediate seat-sponsorship outreach.

### 4. 12-Month Social Media Autopsy
*Audited Afriment's LinkedIn performance to optimize content formats for maximum conversion.*
![Social Media Autopsy](assets/slides/Slide_Social_Autopsy.png)
**Strategic Outcome:** Proved that while the market relies on single images (yielding a low 0.20% engagement rate), Documents and Carousels drive up to 5x higher click-through rates. Shifted strategy toward named graduate outcomes and career advice carousels.

## Leadership & Data Governance
As Assistant Team Lead, navigating the technical execution was only half the mandate. Managing the human element of data strategy included:
* **Enforcing Quality Gates:** Systematically auditing datasets for structural anomalies and managing team attendance to ensure optimal velocity.
* **Operational Deadlines:** Driving the team through strict 17:00 WAT data freezes to ensure models were locked before synthesis.
* **Executive Synthesis:** Bridging the gap between raw data engineering and high-level business storytelling, ensuring our presentation architects had a bulletproof, mathematically certain narrative for the final stakeholder defense.
